# Docked chat toggle

Toggle the docked AI chat from the board change list (rail).

You can also open `/chat` or the AI chat rail on the change list, tracked by `Ctrl+Shift+?`, and close it with the normal close buttons.

On the board change list, reached with the digits `5` or `Ctrl+Shift+?`, a toggle shows or hides the docked AI chat. Click the eye or crossed-eye icon to open or close the chat. The toggle also drives the rail: when the chat is closed, the rail hides it, and when it is open, the toggle opens or closes the chat panel.
